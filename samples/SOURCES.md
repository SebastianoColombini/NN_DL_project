# Sample images

The images in this folder are a small subset of the **MIT1003** saliency dataset, the test
set used in the project. Each stimulus `<id>.jpeg` comes with its continuous fixation map
`<id>_fixMap.jpg` (ground truth), taken unchanged from `ALLSTIMULI.zip` and
`ALLFIXATIONMAPS.zip`.

- T. Judd, K. Ehinger, F. Durand, A. Torralba. *Learning to Predict Where Humans Look*.
  IEEE International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV), 2009.
- Dataset page: http://people.csail.mit.edu/tjudd/WherePeopleLook/

The images are redistributed here only as examples for the GUI, for research and teaching
purposes, with credit to the original authors. `samples.json` sets their order and titles
in the GUI.
